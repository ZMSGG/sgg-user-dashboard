# Responsive Image 2.0 Recomposition

The initial concept prompts are preserved in `PROMPTS.md`. The ten selected
candidate files were then substantively rebuilt with the Codex built-in
OpenAI Image 2.0 tool. They are not crops, resizes, or direct copies.

## Input order

1. The matching initial Image 2.0 concept candidate
2. Canonical Creator Kit GOD main illustration
3. Canonical Creator Kit GOD front identity sheet
4. Canonical Creator Kit OTOMO image in the named form

## Shared final prompt

```text

master while retaining its specific scene concept, premium Japanese
mythic-futurist anime quality, and pair-specific palette. Image 1 is the
existing generated concept and must be used only as scene/style inspiration,
not copied or merely resized. Images 2 and 3 are canonical GOD identity
references. Image 4 is the canonical OTOMO identity reference in the
explicitly named form.

Keep the canvas horizontal 16:9. The real mobile viewport shows approximately
x=41–73% and places headline, copy, and buttons over the lower part. Group the
complete GOD and OTOMO tightly on a small elevated landing platform in the
upper-middle scene. Keep both faces and the exact OTOMO form simultaneously
readable in that narrow portrait crop. Keep the left side dark and low-detail
for desktop copy and keep the lower area dark and free of identity-critical
details. Do not draw crop guides or safe-area marks.

Exactly two characters total. Preserve canonical faces, age, body proportions,
costume colors and motifs, silhouette, equipment, and exact OTOMO form. No
extra people, creatures, silhouettes, duplicates, crowds, new props, readable
symbols, text, letters, numbers, logos, UI, watermark, or signature.
Family-friendly, non-sexualized, and anatomically coherent.
```
